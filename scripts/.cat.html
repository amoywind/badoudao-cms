Wood Wall Clocks · Wholesale & OEM

Wood Wall Clocks — wholesale & OEM manufacturer

Oak, walnut and bamboo, CNC-engraved and finished in wood-wax oil. Solid wood moves with the weather, so the moisture content of every board is held between 8% and 12% before it is cut — that is what keeps a clock from cracking in a dry warehouse or swelling in a damp container. The movement is a silent sweep, and the hands are matched to the grain of the case. We hold solid wood, veneer and MDF bodies, and the double-sided corner clocks are made in the same tool room.
